## How to sign

For now, signing is by assertion: your pull request description (or a commit
trailer) must include the line below, which certifies the above and the
[Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/):

```
I have read and agree to the CLA (CLA.md).  Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

Add the sign-off automatically with `git commit -s`. If a CLA-assistant bot is
later enabled on the repository, it will record acceptance on your first PR
instead.

If anything here is a blocker for you or your employer, open an issue — we would
rather find a workable arrangement than lose your contribution.
